Pie

Description:

This is a delicious pie recipe that everyone will love!

Ingredients:

  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, chilled and diced
  • 1/4 cup ice water
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 4 cups fresh or frozen fruit (such as apples, berries, or peaches)
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon butter, cut into small pieces

Steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C).
  2. In a large bowl, combine flour and salt. Cut in butter until mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
  3. Gradually add ice water, mixing until dough forms a ball.
  4. Wrap dough in plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes.
  5. Roll out dough on a floured surface to fit a 9-inch pie plate.
  6. Transfer dough to pie plate and trim edges.
  7. In a large bowl, combine sugar, flour, cinnamon, and nutmeg.
  8. Add fruit and lemon juice, tossing to coat.
  9. Pour fruit mixture into crust.
  10. Dot with butter pieces.
  11. Bake for 45 minutes or until crust is golden brown and filling is bubbly.
